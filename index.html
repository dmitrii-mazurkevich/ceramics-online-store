From the studio

Three firings and one pair of hands

Each piece is thrown on the wheel, dried for a week, bisque fired, glazed by dipping and fired again at 1240 °C. We make small batches, so a glaze can sell out and come back a month later with a slightly different shade.

  1. Throw and trimon the wheel, by hand
  2. Bisque firingto make the clay strong
  3. Glaze and fireat 1240 °C, food safe
